Frequently asked questions

What services do you provide?

Document preparation, graphic design, online registration help, application document support, web design, data analysis and ICT support. See the Services page.

How do I request a service?

Use the WhatsApp button or the enquiry form, describe what you need, and we will reply.

How are prices determined?

Prices depend on the scope of each job. We discuss scope and price with you before work begins.

Can you assist customers outside Nigeria?

Yes. We work remotely with clients worldwide. Enquire with your requirements.

What should I include in a quote request?

The service you want, a short description, any deadline, and any requirements or templates supplied by the institution or employer.

Do you guarantee application or registration approval?

No. Decisions are made by the relevant institution or authority. We help you prepare and present truthful information well.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the work. We confirm a timeline with you once the scope is clear.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Revision terms are agreed with you when the scope is confirmed.
